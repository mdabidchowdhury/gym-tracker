# Gym Tracker

A gym-tracker app personalized for me, because why not? Offline-first PWA for short home workouts: bodyweight, a backpack and water bottles.

## What it does
- **Two full-body sessions (A/B)** that alternate whenever you train, plus an 8-minute **floor session** for busy weeks. A missed day never skips a body part.
- **Logs reps, backpack load and effort** for every set, pre-filled from last time, and suggests when to move to a harder variation (two sessions at the top of the rep range).
- **Rest and hold timers** with vibration. The screen stays on during a session.
- **Weekly goal and streak.** A floor session keeps the streak alive, and you can pause a week when sick or travelling.
- **Sets per muscle each week**, with the 4-set minimum marked.
- **Body numbers:** weight with a 7-day average, waist-to-height ratio, steps with a personal target, and optional protein and sleep.
- **History calendar:** view, edit, backfill or delete any session.
- **Backups:** share or download a JSON file; import v1 or v2 backups.
